Coaching for someone on your team

For HR and learning and development teams sponsoring coaching, and for managers approving it from a team budget.

Who it suits

A new manager in their first year

Someone good at the work who now has to lead the people doing it. The first year sets habits that last.

A leader stepping up or leading through change

A bigger role, a new team, or a reorganisation to carry through, with nobody neutral to think it through with.

Someone you are letting go

People leaving deserve good support. Individual outplacement coaching helps them work out what comes next and land it.

How it works

A three-way conversation at the start

The sponsor, the person being coached and I meet to agree the objectives. That gives everyone the same picture of what the coaching is for and what progress looks like.

Confidentiality, plainly

The sponsor gets progress against the objectives we agreed. The sponsor does not get the content of sessions. What someone says in a session stays between them and me, and everyone knows that from the start. It is what makes the coaching work.

A written mid-point review

For The Leadership Engagement, I write a mid-point review of progress against the objectives, shared as agreed in the three-way conversation.

Two forms of The Next Move for employers

New-manager coaching

For learning and development teams supporting a manager in their first year. Six sessions over twelve weeks, a transition map and a written 90 day plan. €1,200.

Individual outplacement

For someone leaving the organisation. Six sessions over twelve weeks on what comes next: their story, CV and LinkedIn, interviews and the offer. €1,200.

For more senior people, The Leadership Engagement runs over six months and includes structured feedback at the start and a written mid-point review.
