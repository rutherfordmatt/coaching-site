For organisations

Coaching for someone on your team

For HR and learning and development teams sponsoring coaching, and for managers approving it from a team budget.

Request a proposal

Who it suits

  • A new manager in their first year

    Someone good at the work who now has to lead the people doing it. The first year sets habits that last.

  • A leader stepping up or leading through change

    A bigger role, a new team, or a reorganisation to carry through, with nobody neutral to think it through with.

  • Someone you are letting go

    People leaving deserve good support. Individual outplacement coaching helps them work out what comes next and land it.

What you can choose

  • New-manager coaching

    The Next Move for a manager in their first year: six sessions over twelve weeks, a transition map and a written 90 day plan. €1,200.

  • Individual outplacement

    The Next Move for someone leaving: six sessions over twelve weeks on what comes next, from their story, CV and LinkedIn to interviews and the offer. €1,200.

  • Leadership coaching

    The Leadership Engagement for more senior people: six months, structured feedback at the start and a written mid-point review. €3,000.

Confidentiality, plainly

You get progress against the objectives we agree together. You do not get the content of sessions. What someone says in a session stays between them and me, and everyone knows that from the start. It is what makes the coaching work.

For The Leadership Engagement, I also write a mid-point review of progress against the objectives, shared as agreed at the start.

Cost and invoicing

The same published prices as for individuals: there is one price list, whoever pays. For more than one person, I will put together a single proposal.

I invoice the organisation directly, payable by bank transfer or card. If you need a purchase order number on the invoice, send it with the go-ahead and I will include it.

How it starts

Three steps to getting started

  1. 1

    Proposal

    In writing

    Tell me about the person and what you want the coaching to achieve. I reply with a short written proposal.

  2. 2

    Three-way conversation

    Objectives agreed

    You, the person being coached and I agree the objectives and what progress looks like.

  3. 3

    Start

    Within two weeks

    Coaching begins, with the first session within two weeks.
