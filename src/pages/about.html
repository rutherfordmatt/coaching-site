Why coaching

The part of that work I valued most was always the one to one conversations. A manager working out how to lead the team they used to be part of. A leader with a decision nobody else could make for them. Someone whose role had just disappeared. Good support at those moments changes what happens next, and coaching lets me give it properly.

Who I work with
