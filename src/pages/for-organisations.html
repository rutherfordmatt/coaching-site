How it works

A three-way conversation at the start

The sponsor, the person being coached and I meet to agree the objectives. That gives everyone the same picture of what the coaching is for and what progress looks like.

Confidentiality, plainly

The sponsor gets progress against the objectives we agreed. The sponsor does not get the content of sessions. What someone says in a session stays between them and me, and everyone knows that from the start. It is what makes the coaching work.

A written mid-point review

For The Leadership Engagement, I write a mid-point review of progress against the objectives, shared as agreed in the three-way conversation.
