Who it is for

Some things do not need a programme. You have an offer to weigh, a conversation to prepare for, a restructure to respond to, or a choice you keep putting off. The Clarity Session gives it ninety minutes of proper attention with someone who has no stake in what you decide.

Before

You send a short written brief: the decision or situation, what you have already considered, and what a good outcome would look like.

After
