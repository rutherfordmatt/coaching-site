Who it is for

Some people want a regular, neutral place to think out loud about their work, with no fixed programme. Others want to try a session or two before they commit to anything longer. Ongoing Partnership works for both.

Paying

Single sessions and blocks are payable when you book; if you would rather pay by bank transfer, say so and I will send an invoice instead.

If you have completed The Next Move or The Leadership Engagement, you continue at an alumni rate: blocks of four for €640 or eight for €1,280, which is €160 a session. It is the same work with someone who already knows your map.

Sessions or a programme?

Six single sessions cost the same as The Next Move, which also includes the transition map, the workbook and a written 90 day plan. If you have a specific move in mind, the programme is usually the better choice. We can talk it through in the first conversation.
