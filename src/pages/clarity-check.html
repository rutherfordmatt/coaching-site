How it works

Twelve questions, three parts.

  1. Part one

    Where you are

    Your situation, how long it has been on your mind, and who you have talked it through with.

  2. Part two

    What is in the way

    What happens when you try to move on it, and where it tends to get stuck.

  3. Part three

    What you want

    Two short answers in your own words, and how soon you want to move. They stay in your browser and appear on your result.
