How it runs

The start

I gather structured feedback from your manager, peers and team, so the map of your situation is built on more than your own view. We use it to set what the six months are for.

The middle

Fortnightly sessions while there is most to work on, then monthly. At the mid-point I write a short review of progress against what we set out to do.

The close

A written close-out: what changed, what is next, and what to keep doing.
