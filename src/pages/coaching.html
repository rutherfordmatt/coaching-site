Prices and payment

How paying works

Paying

The conversation is free. The Clarity Session and single sessions are paid by card when you book.

Programmes and session blocks are invoiced after the first conversation and paid by bank transfer or card. If you pay in instalments, each one is a dated invoice.

Sessions or a programme?

Six single sessions cost the same as The Next Move, which also includes the transition map, the workbook and a written 90 day plan.

If you have a specific move in mind, the programme is usually the better choice.

If your employer pays

The prices are the same. We agree objectives together at the start, and what we discuss stays confidential.
