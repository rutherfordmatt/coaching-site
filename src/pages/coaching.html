Prices and payment

Why my prices are on the page

Most coaches at this level don't publish prices. I do, for three reasons.

You should be able to decide whether coaching is worth it before you talk to me, not after. A price on the page lets you weigh it up in your own time and come to the first conversation already knowing it fits. That makes the conversation about you, not about money.

One price list, whoever pays. If your employer funds the coaching, they pay what you would. There is no corporate rate, because the work is the same.

These are opening rates. I'm building a new practice and I'd rather work with more people at a fair price than fewer at a premium one. I'll review them in January 2027, and any change applies to new engagements only. Whatever you start at, you finish at.

How you pay. Our first conversation is free. The Clarity Session and single sessions are paid by card when you book; if you would rather pay by bank transfer, say so and I'll send an invoice instead. Programmes and session blocks are invoiced after the first conversation and paid by bank transfer or card, with instalments as dated invoices: two for The Next Move, three for The Leadership Engagement.
