Working with me

The short version: who I work best with, how to tell if we are a fit, and what our first conversation is for. For a neutral guide, read Five questions to ask before hiring a career coach.

Who I work best with

People making progress in their careers, from senior individual contributor to senior leader. Usually in the middle of a change:

  • stepping up to manage people
  • starting a new role
  • looking for the next one, including after a layoff
  • deciding whether to stay

How I work

I listen first. I tell you what I see, plainly. You leave each session with something to try. The decisions stay yours.

Probably a fit if

  • you want a thinking partner who will be honest with you
  • you are ready to try things between sessions

Probably not a fit if

  • you want someone to find you a job
  • you want one fixed method, whatever your situation

The first conversation

A Discovery Session: 30 minutes, online. It is on me, and it is not a sales call. You tell me where you are and what you want to change. We both find out if we would work well together. If we would, I send a short proposal. If not, I will say so and suggest someone who might be a better fit.
