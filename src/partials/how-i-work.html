How I work

A map first, then a plan

In the first session we map your situation. Every session after that works a part of the map, so you leave with a written plan rather than a set of notes.

  • Where you are

    Your role, your strengths, and what is and is not working right now.

  • Where you are going

    The move you want to make, described specifically enough to plan for.

  • What is in the way

    The gaps, habits and circumstances that slow you down.

  • Who matters

    The people whose support, decisions or opinion will shape the move.

The coaching follows the Association for Coaching code of ethics.
