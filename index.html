How I Can Help

Three ways to work together

Whether you're leading a business, navigating a career crossroads, or just need space to think, there's a way of working that fits where you are.

01

Business Coaching

A structured 1:1 engagement built around you and what you want to achieve. Clear goals, honest conversations, and real accountability to help you lead with more impact.

  • Leadership presence and impact
  • Strategic thinking and decision-making
  • Your first 90 days in a new role
  • Building confidence under pressure
02

Career Coaching

Whether you're going for promotion, searching for a new role, facing redundancy or retirement. I'll help you get clarity for the next move you make.

  • Clarity of direction and next steps
  • Job search strategy and positioning
  • Interview and presentation coaching
  • Redundancy, promotion and retirement
03

Thought Partnership

A flexible, high-trust space to think out loud with someone who has seen it all. No formal programme, no fixed agenda. Just a candid thinking partner when you need one.

  • Confidential space to think clearly
  • Sounding board for big decisions
  • Navigating complexity and uncertainty
  • Fresh perspective on hard problems

The Approach

Simple. Focused. Effective.

Every engagement is different, but the core of my work follows a clear path from where you are to where you want to be.

About Me

30 years at the intersection of technology and leadership

I've spent three decades working in organisations where the pace is relentless, the stakes are high, and the space to think clearly is rare. I've seen first-hand how the best leaders aren't always the ones with the most experience. They're the ones who know how to get out of their own way.

I work with people who are ready to think more clearly, lead more effectively, and move forward with real purpose. My approach is direct, practical, and built around you, not a formula. I'm a registered coach, though the 30 years in the room tends to matter more.
