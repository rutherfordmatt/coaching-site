The Next Move

For people making a specific career move: stepping up into management, landing the next role, or starting well in a new one.

€1,200or two payments of €600

Length
Six 60 minute sessions over twelve weeks
Payment
Invoiced after the first conversation. One payment, or two of €600.
Start with a free conversation

Who it is for

People about to manage for the first time, new and recent managers, and experienced professionals between roles. You have a specific move to make, and you want to make it well rather than just get through it.

What is included

Six 60 minute sessions over twelve weeks, and:

  • A transition map of your situation, built in the first session
  • A workbook between sessions
  • Messaging access between sessions
  • A written 90 day plan you leave with

Three tracks

In the first session we build your transition map. Then we choose the track together.

Stepping up

Your first management role, from before you start through your first year, including your first 90 days. New to managing? Read how to start well.

Landing the next role

Your story, your CV and LinkedIn, interviews, and the offer. Looking by choice? Read finding your next role. Just been laid off? Read what to do this week.

Starting well

You have been promoted, moved sideways, or joined a new employer. Read how to use your first 90 days.

Paying

€1,200, or two payments of €600. Invoiced after the first conversation and paid by bank transfer or card. Each instalment is a dated invoice.

For employers

The Next Move is also available to employers in two forms: as new-manager coaching for learning and development teams, and as individual outplacement support for someone leaving the organisation. The price is the same.
