Coaching package

Ongoing Partnership

For people who want a standing thinking partner, or who want to start with a session or two before committing to a programme.

Book a free conversation See the packages

Who it is for

Some people want a regular, neutral place to think out loud about their work, with no fixed programme. Others want to try a session or two before they commit to anything longer. Ongoing Partnership works for both.

What is included

  • 60 minute sessions, booked singly or in blocks of four (€720) or eight (€1,360)
  • Blocks are valid for six months
  • Blocks include messaging between sessions and priority scheduling

Prices

  • Single session: €200, paid by card when you book.
  • Block of four: €720, invoiced.
  • Block of eight: €1,360, invoiced.

Blocks are paid by bank transfer or card, and are valid for six months.

Sessions or a programme?

Six single sessions cost the same as The Next Move, which also includes the transition map, the workbook and a written 90 day plan. If you have a specific move in mind, the programme is usually the better choice. We can talk it through in the free conversation.
