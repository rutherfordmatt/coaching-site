Who it is for

Some people want a regular, neutral place to think out loud about their work, with no fixed programme. Others want to try a session or two before they commit to anything longer. Coaching Sessions work for both.

What is included

  • 60 minute sessions, booked singly or in blocks
  • Priority scheduling and messaging between sessions with a block
  • A block of four is valid for six months, a block of eight for 12 months

Paying

Single sessions and blocks are payable when you book; if you would rather pay by bank transfer, say so and I will send an invoice instead.

If you have completed The Next Move or The Leadership Engagement, you continue at an alumni rate: blocks of four for €640 or eight for €1,280, which is €160 a session. It is the same work with someone who already knows your map.

Sessions or a programme?

Six single sessions cost the same as The Next Move, which also includes the transition map, the workbook and a written 90 day plan. If you have a specific move in mind, the programme is usually the better choice. We can talk it through in a Discovery Session.

Questions people ask

How often are sessions?

As often as suits you. Some people book every two weeks, others once a month or when something comes up. A block of four is valid for six months and a block of eight for 12 months, so you can set your own pace.

Should I book a block or single sessions?

Single sessions suit you if you want to try coaching, or only need to think something through now and then. A block of four saves 10% and a block of eight saves 15%, and both include messaging between sessions and priority scheduling.

What can we talk about?

Whatever is on your mind at work: a decision, a difficult relationship, a change you are leading, or where your career is going. There is no fixed programme; you bring the agenda.

When is a programme the better choice?

When you have a specific move to make. Six single sessions cost the same as The Next Move, which also includes the transition map, the workbook and a written 90 day plan.
