Who it is for

Some things do not need a programme. You have an offer to weigh, a conversation to prepare for, a restructure to respond to, or a choice you keep putting off. The Clarity Session gives it ninety minutes of proper attention with someone who has no stake in what you decide.

What is included

  • A short written brief from you beforehand
  • One 90 minute session
  • A written summary with the decision, options and next steps
  • A 30 minute follow-up call three weeks later

How it works

Before

You send a short written brief: the decision or situation, what you have already considered, and what a good outcome would look like.

The session

Ninety minutes, online, working it through properly.

After

I send a written summary with the decision, the options and your next steps.

Three weeks later

A 30 minute follow-up call to see what has moved.

If you want to keep going

The fee is credited against The Next Move or The Leadership Engagement if you continue within 30 days.
